Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026. Operator: the owner of WHY ON EARTH?, reachable at alan689becker@gmail.com.

Data accessed and purpose

With the owner's authorization, this personal assistant accesses YouTube channel information, video metadata, publication settings and available statistics to provide channel-management and editorial analysis. If YouTube Analytics permission is granted, it may also access authorized performance reports such as views, watch time and audience information. Access depends on the scopes actually granted and the API's capabilities.

Processing and storage

The tool operates in an Arena.ai workspace. OAuth credentials are stored in credential files there; retrieved data, audits and backups may be stored in workspace files. Data supplied to the assistant may be processed by Arena.ai and its service providers under their applicable terms and privacy policies. The operator does not control all platform-level retention or backup behavior. Credential files must not be uploaded to this public website or its repository.

The static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own privacy policy. These pages contain no advertising trackers or custom analytics scripts.

Use and disclosure

Google user data is used to provide the channel-management and analysis features described here, not sold or used by the operator for advertising. Requested changes to video titles, descriptions, thumbnails or other public channel fields may become public on YouTube. Public information pages are stored in the public website repository; private channel reports and credentials are not intended for that repository.

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The operator must ensure that any downstream processing complies with these requirements before transmitting private Google user data.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Workspace credentials and reports may remain until removed by the operator; this tool does not currently enforce a fixed automatic deletion schedule. The owner can revoke access at Google Account permissions. Revocation prevents further authorized access but does not automatically delete previously saved reports or change data already on YouTube. To request removal of application-held files, contact alan689becker@gmail.com. Copies held by hosting or platform providers are subject to their own retention policies.

Questions or updates

Contact alan689becker@gmail.com. Material changes to this application or its data handling should be reflected in this policy before the new processing is enabled.
